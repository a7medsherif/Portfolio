Business Problem & Solution

The Problem

Furniture orders were recorded across detailed rows, making it hard to compare which product categories and sales platforms drove the most orders, where customers were concentrated, and whether deliveries met their target dates. The project brief focused on finding the best- and lowest-selling products.

The Solution

I built an interactive Excel dashboard with slicers for sales platform, product category, customer type, and governorate. PivotTables and charts summarize product and color demand, platform mix, order outcomes, and delivery timing.

  • Compare product categories and sales channels to support stock and assortment decisions.
  • Track delivered, rejected, and returned orders alongside on-time delivery.
  • Filter demand by customer type and governorate to guide follow-up analysis.

What the Analysis Shows

Product Demand

Beds lead with 88 records (69.8%), followed by sofas with 18 (14.3%) and tables with 17 (13.5%). The remaining categories account for 3 records.

Delivery Outcomes

102 records are marked delivered (81%), 23 rejected (18.3%), and 1 returned (0.8%). The timing field marks 70 on time and 56 late.

Channel Mix

Homzmart accounts for 78 records (61.9%), Chic Homes for 16 (12.7%), and Oscar Riten for 13 (10.3%). The top three platforms make up about 85% of records.

Customer & Color Mix

The workbook contains 78 distinct customer names: 75 individuals and 3 companies. Black is the most frequent metal-color entry, followed by white (37) and gray (8).

Analysis limitation: The workbook records rejected and delayed orders but does not include a rejection-reason field. The dashboard can show where outcomes differ, but it cannot establish why an order was rejected without additional data.
