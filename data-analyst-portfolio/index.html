About Me

Bridging the gap between raw data and executive business decisions.

I am a results-driven Data Analyst with a genuine curiosity for uncovering the story behind every dataset. In today’s fast-moving business landscape, intuition alone isn't enough; my mission is to eliminate ambiguity and empower stakeholders with actionable, data-backed insights.

I thrive on the end-to-end analytical workflow: writing optimized SQL queries to pull and normalize data, utilizing Python for exploratory data analysis and data cleansing, and crafting clean, intuitive interactive dashboards in Power BI and Tableau that make complex patterns immediately clear.

Data Extraction & Cleaning

Ensuring data integrity through schema normalization, outlier handling, and structured SQL pipelines.

Exploratory & Statistical Analysis

Formulating hypotheses, segmenting customer cohorts, and diagnosing performance trends.

Storytelling & Visual BI

Designing interactive executive dashboards with clear visual hierarchy, drill-downs, and actionable KPIs.
